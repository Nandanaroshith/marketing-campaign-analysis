# Marketing Campaign Analysis | Python

## Project Overview

Analyzed marketing campaign data using Python to understand customer behavior, evaluate marketing channel performance, and explore factors influencing customer spending and revenue generation. The project uses exploratory data analysis (EDA) to identify patterns and derive actionable business insights.

## Objectives

- Analyze customer demographics, including age, income, region, and customer segments.
- Compare marketing channel performance across Email, YouTube, TikTok, Google Ads, and Instagram.
- Explore customer engagement, campaign responses, and discount usage.
- Examine spending patterns and revenue generation across customer segments and marketing channels.
- Identify trends and relationships in the data to support data-driven marketing decisions.

## Tools and Technologies

- **Python** – Data analysis and manipulation
- **Pandas** – Data cleaning and data manipulation
- **NumPy** – Numerical operations
- **Matplotlib and Seaborn** – Data visualization
- **Jupyter Notebook** – Exploratory data analysis

## Key Analysis Areas

- Customer demographics and regional analysis
- Marketing channel performance comparison
- Customer segmentation and purchasing behavior
- Campaign engagement and response analysis
- Revenue and spending pattern analysis
- Correlation analysis between customer spending and revenue

## Key Insights

- The Central region recorded the highest average income, while the North region recorded the lowest.
- YouTube and TikTok had nearly equal customer counts, indicating similar audience reach in the dataset.
- Email marketing generated the highest revenue among the analyzed channels, while YouTube generated the lowest.
- Google Ads generated moderate revenue compared with the other analyzed channels.
- The correlation analysis indicated a negative relationship between customer spending and revenue in the analyzed dataset, suggesting an area for further investigation.

## Project Workflow

1. Data loading and inspection
2. Data cleaning and preprocessing
3. Exploratory data analysis (EDA)
4. Customer segmentation and marketing channel analysis
5. Data visualization and correlation analysis
6. Business insights and conclusions

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates the application of Python-based data analytics to understand customer behavior, compare marketing channel performance, and explore revenue patterns. The findings help identify potential opportunities for customer targeting, marketing strategy improvement, and data-driven decision-making.
